# QUN Mauritius — legal and compliance checklist

This is issue-spotting, not legal advice. Confirm current rules with qualified Mauritius counsel and relevant authorities before operating.

## Operating model
The intended model is a local chapter of the international QUN ecosystem. “Chapter” describes the operating concept, not a legal status. Compare operating through the existing QUN owner/entity, a registered branch if appropriate, or a Mauritius-incorporated subsidiary. Compare IP ownership, tax, control, liability, banking, substance and recurring costs before deciding.

## Trademark and IP
Official starting point: Mauritius Industrial Property Office and Industrial Property Act 2019: https://foreign.govmu.org/Pages/Industrial%20Property%20Office/ABOUT-US0409-2313.aspx
- Search QUN, QUN Mauritius, QUN Messenger, QUN Market, QUN AI, QUN Wallet, QUN Hub, logo and phonetic variants.
- Confirm Nice classes against actual products and services.
- Check registered/pending marks, company names, domains and social handles.
- Document global owner and chain of title; review contributor assignments and third-party assets.
- Compare national filing with Madrid System options with counsel. Do not claim the mark is available until reviewed.

## Data protection
Official starting point: Mauritius Data Protection Office https://dataprotection.govmu.org/
Review the Data Protection Act 2017 and current guidance with counsel: controller/processor roles, lawful basis, privacy notice, rights and retention; children's data; cookies and marketing consent; security and breach response; hosting, subprocessors and cross-border transfers; deletion and backups. Use data minimization and privacy by design.

## Marketplace, consumer and payments
Confirm business registration, tax/VAT, invoicing, seller verification, prohibited goods, listing moderation, pricing, cancellation/refunds, warranties, delivery, settlement, chargebacks, electronic contracts, advertising and dispute handling. Do not hold customer funds or present QUN as a payment provider without legal and partner approval.

## Crypto and virtual-asset services
Official starting point: Financial Services Commission Mauritius https://www.fscmauritius.org/
The Virtual Asset and Initial Token Offering Services Act 2021 may apply depending on token and services. Obtain written advice from Mauritius counsel before issuance or operation. Questions include token classification, offering approval, exchange/brokerage/custody/transfer/advisory licensing, AML/CFT, due diligence, sanctions, recordkeeping, marketing disclosures, tax and cross-border obligations.

No public token offering, custody, exchange listing, investment solicitation or paid financial functionality until the legal pathway is documented and explicitly approved. Never promise profit or token appreciation.

## Required before public launch
- Trademark clearance memo and filing recommendation.
- Operating model and tax/accounting review.
- Privacy/data-flow map and privacy notice.
- Terms, seller terms and acceptable-use policy.
- Payment partner approval if payments are offered.
- Crypto classification/licensing memo if crypto is proposed.
- Security review, incident-response plan, approved budget and accountable operators.
