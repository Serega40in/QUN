# QUN Mauritius — roadmap

This is a gated plan. No stage authorizes spending, incorporation, token issuance or public financial services by itself.

## Stage 0 — establish the initiative
- [x] Confirm local chapter of the international QUN ecosystem.
- [x] Create initial planning documentation on a feature branch.
- [ ] Review and merge documentation through the normal QUN PR process.
- [ ] Assign an accountable product lead and local research contact.
Exit: ownership, scope and decision process are clear.

## Stage 1 — discover the market
- [ ] Verify at least 10 competitors/services across messaging, commerce, payments, AI and developer tools.
- [ ] Inspect availability, features, fees, languages, onboarding and trust features.
- [ ] Interview at least 5 potential users/businesses where feasible, with consent and anonymized notes.
- [ ] Identify one repeated, important unmet need.
- [ ] Compare 2–3 pilot candidates by demand, time, cost, compliance and differentiation.
Exit: evidence-based product recommendation.

## Stage 2 — legal and brand review
- [ ] Search QUN and product marks in Mauritius and relevant international registers.
- [ ] Review trademark ownership, classes and filing route with IP counsel.
- [ ] Compare chapter, branch and subsidiary models without premature incorporation.
- [ ] Review privacy, consumer, marketplace and payment requirements.
- [ ] Obtain written legal classification before any token/regulated crypto service.
- [ ] Estimate recurring costs; no paid commitments without explicit approval.
Exit: written legal recommendations and approved operating model.

## Stage 3 — validate one pilot
Possible candidates after discovery: local-business storefront/catalogue; multilingual AI content assistant; community/messaging workflow; developer onboarding/API integrations.
- [ ] Define target segment and measurable hypothesis.
- [ ] Prototype with shared QUN components where safe.
- [ ] Test with a small opt-in cohort.
- [ ] Measure activation, repeat use, user value, support burden and cost.
- [ ] Provide privacy notice, terms and feedback channel.
Exit: evidence of repeated value and a safe operating path.

## Stage 4 — public launch decision
Complete security, privacy, legal and localization review; confirm support and incident response; approve budget and business model; publish only verified capabilities; set monitoring, rollback and abuse-reporting procedures.

## Stage 5 — ecosystem expansion
Expand to Messenger, Market, AI, ID, Hub, Publisher, Search, Node and Storage in an order based on demand and readiness. QUN Wallet/token remains behind a separate legal approval gate.
