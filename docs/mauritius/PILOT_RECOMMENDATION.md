# QUN Mauritius — initial pilot recommendation

**Status:** preliminary recommendation based on desk research only. No user interviews have been completed. Treat this as a hypothesis to test, not a final investment decision.

## Recommendation
Do not start by launching a new all-in-one marketplace, general-purpose messenger, or token. Run discovery for a narrow **QUN Local Business Digital Kit**: a lightweight multilingual business profile/catalogue plus shareable product/service links and a structured enquiry path. Reuse existing QUN web and identity components where technically suitable; do not require a new account or payment integration until tests show it is necessary.

Possible initial functions for a prototype:
- English-first interface with a reviewed French version and tested Kreol Morisien content;
- shareable mobile storefront/catalogue;
- simple enquiry button that routes to a channel chosen by the business;
- clear opening hours, location/service area, contact and privacy information;
- optional AI-assisted draft descriptions as a later opt-in feature, with human review.

This is not a commitment to build or publish yet. Existing products already cover local marketplace listings and messaging, and Mauritius has an AI solution marketplace plus local business software. QUN must validate a specific workflow gap before coding beyond a prototype.

## Why this is the first hypothesis
- It can be tested with a small group without needing a large consumer network on day one.
- It avoids holding customer funds and can defer payment processing.
- It can reuse global QUN technology and brand rather than creating a disconnected service.
- It may support a later path into QUN Market, AI, Messenger and QUN ID if repeat usage is demonstrated.

## Alternatives compared
| Pilot | Potential upside | Main risk | Current decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| General marketplace | Broad ambition and visible local use case | Direct competitors already exist; trust, supply/demand balance, moderation, disputes and logistics are hard | Do not lead with this until a specific underserved segment is proven |
| New messenger | Fits QUN ecosystem | Network effects and switching costs are substantial; existing channels are entrenched | Defer unless a concrete community/business workflow is validated |
| Token / wallet | Potential ecosystem utility | Legal classification/licensing, AML/CFT, custody/security, consumer risk and cost | Separate legal gate; not an MVP |
| Local Business Digital Kit | Narrow scope, shareable, potentially low-friction | Existing website/social/catalogue tools may be sufficient; willingness to adopt is unproven | Test with interviews and prototype before build |
| AI-assisted business content | Potentially fast to prototype | Global tools and local providers already exist; value and language quality need validation | Candidate add-on, not yet standalone product |

## Validation plan
1. Interview at least five local businesses and five residents if feasible; use USER_DISCOVERY.md. Never invent interview results.
2. Inspect at least ten alternatives, including marketplace apps, social channels, website builders, business software and payment providers.
3. Ask 3–5 businesses to test a clickable or manually assembled prototype with their own sample catalogue data.
4. Observe whether they can publish/share a profile and receive a useful enquiry without assistance.
5. Ask whether they would keep using it and what current tool they would replace; do not rely on compliments alone.

## Suggested go/no-go criteria (agree before testing)
- At least 5 target businesses describe the same recurring problem from recent experience.
- At least 3 businesses complete a prototype task without hands-on help.
- At least 3 agree to a time-bounded pilot and name a real workflow they will use it for.
- No unresolved critical privacy, safety or legal issue.
- Hosting and support cost can be estimated and fits an explicitly approved budget.

These are initial decision thresholds, not research results or promises.

## Cost and safety constraints
- Start with static or existing infrastructure if it meets requirements; no new paid services by default.
- No payment custody, public token, investment claims or token sale.
- Use demo/sample data until privacy and security requirements are approved.
- No domain purchases or company-registration commitments without explicit approval.

## Human decisions required after discovery
1. Confirm the pilot segment and exact problem.
2. Approve prototype scope and any spend limit.
3. Confirm who will conduct local interviews and review English/French/Kreol Morisien content.
