# QUN Mauritius — pilot recommendation

**Status:** preliminary desk-research hypothesis. No interviews completed; no product launch authorized.

## Recommendation
Do not begin with a general marketplace, generic messenger or token. First validate a **QUN Business Workflow Kit**: a small tool that helps a selected type of Mauritian small business keep its public business information and product/service catalogue consistent across the places customers already use. The first prototype may be a shareable business page plus a simple enquiry route; only add AI or integrations if discovery shows a specific recurring pain.

This is deliberately narrower than “another marketplace”. MoBazar, Maubazaar, MauMarket, FLIQO, mowebstore and other platforms already cover substantial parts of local selling, messaging, payments, delivery or AI-assisted commerce. The pilot must prove a gap rather than assume one.

## Prototype boundary
- Mobile-first shareable profile/catalogue using sample or public business data initially.
- English UI baseline; French and Kreol Morisien copy reviewed by fluent local speakers before public use.
- Enquiry link chosen by the business (no forced new messaging network).
- Optional draft product/service description as a later opt-in; user reviews before publishing.
- No payment processing, escrow, user accounts, customer database, or token in the first prototype.
- Clear demo label and no claim that the product is live or available to the public.

## Alternatives compared
| Candidate | Advantage | Main risk | Current stance |
|---|---|---|---|
| General marketplace | Broad use case | Existing competitors, two-sided cold start, trust/disputes/delivery | Defer until underserved niche proven |
| New messenger | Fits global QUN vision | Network effects, switching cost, moderation and security | Defer; test a concrete workflow first |
| QUN Wallet/token | Could later support ecosystem utility | Licensing, AML/CFT, custody, security and consumer risk | Separate legal gate; not MVP |
| Business workflow kit | Small, testable and can reuse global QUN web foundation | Existing tools may be sufficient; adoption unproven | Validate through interviews and prototype |
| AI content helper | Fast to prototype | Existing global and local providers; language quality and willingness-to-pay unknown | Feature candidate, not standalone product yet |

## Validation plan
1. Interview at least five local businesses and five residents where feasible; use USER_DISCOVERY.md.
2. Verify at least ten alternatives directly and record actual pricing/onboarding.
3. Select one business segment and one recurring task from evidence.
4. Test a clickable or manually assembled prototype with 3–5 businesses.
5. Measure task completion, time saved, repeat use intent grounded in a real upcoming task, and what existing workaround it would replace.

## Suggested go/no-go criteria (agree before testing)
- At least five independent target businesses describe the same recent recurring problem.
- At least three complete a prototype task without hands-on help.
- At least three agree to a time-bounded pilot and identify a real upcoming use.
- No unresolved critical privacy, security or legal issue.
- Operating cost is estimated and fits a separately approved budget.

These are proposed thresholds, not results or guarantees.

## Guardrails
No paid infrastructure by default. No domain purchase, company registration, token sale, custody, exchange listing, payment handling or financial promotion without explicit approval and legal review. Keep prototype/demo data separate from production. Record assumptions and sources.
