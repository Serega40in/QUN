## Evidence-based conclusions
1. The local commerce ecosystem is already competitive: marketplaces, merchant tools, WhatsApp selling tools, e-commerce providers and payment gateways exist.
2. A general marketplace or general-purpose messenger is not currently justified as the first QUN Mauritius product.
3. A “business catalogue + WhatsApp + AI” pitch is also not unique. Any pilot must be narrower, e.g. cross-channel catalogue maintenance, multilingual content workflow, or an underserved seller segment—only if interviews validate it.
4. Payment methods, merchant eligibility, settlement, refunds and delivery are important product constraints. Avoid taking custody of funds in the first experiment.
5. No reliable market-share or active-user comparisons have been established from this desk scan.
