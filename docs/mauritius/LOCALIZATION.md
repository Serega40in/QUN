# QUN Mauritius — localization guide

## Positioning
QUN Mauritius is the local expression of the international QUN ecosystem. Preserve the global QUN name, visual identity, security principles, shared technology and governance. Localize language, onboarding, support, partnerships, examples, terms and product priorities. Do not imply government endorsement, local incorporation, licensing or product availability until verified.

## Language strategy
- English: initial product and developer-documentation baseline.
- French: reviewed version for relevant audiences and business communication.
- Kreol Morisien (Mauritian Creole): test onboarding, help and community content with native speakers; avoid literal translations.
- Keep UI strings translation-ready and use fluent local reviewers for legal, financial, safety and marketing text.

Before public use, run linguistic, trademark and cultural review plus user testing. Avoid claims such as “the first”, “the safest”, “fully decentralized” or “licensed” unless substantiated.

## UX and design
- Preserve QUN's established blue, emerald/green and gold palette and clean premium style.
- Mobile-first, low-bandwidth friendly pages and progressive loading.
- Show prices and fees clearly in local formats where relevant; verify payment methods with local users.
- Make language switching visible.
- Include accessibility, readable typography, support paths and transparent privacy choices.
- Test on common Android devices and modest network conditions.

Validate preferred language by product and audience, local idioms, support expectations, trust signals, business onboarding and payment/delivery assumptions. Record reviewer and date.
