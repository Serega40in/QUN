# QUN Mauritius — trademark and brand clearance checklist

**Important:** no conclusion has been reached that QUN, the Q logo, or any product name is available for registration in Mauritius. This document is a checklist, not legal advice.

## Official starting points
- Mauritius Industrial Property Office: https://foreign.govmu.org/Pages/Industrial%20Property%20Office/ABOUT-US0409-2313.aspx
- Official trademark fees page: https://foreign.govmu.org/Pages/Industrial%20Property%20Office/Trademark/FEESTrademark.aspx
- Official trademark applicant guidelines (the page itself should be checked for currency): https://foreign.govmu.org/Documents/2020%20-%20migrated%20data/Industrial%20Property%20Office/IPO/Trademark/Guidelines%20Trademarks.pdf

The published fee page currently displays MUR 3,150 for an application covering up to two classes and MUR 1,575 for each additional class, as well as a MUR 210 fee for consultation of registers. The older applicant guideline also mentions a MUR 210 search fee. Because the sources may reflect different rules/versions, confirm the current applicable fees and procedure directly with the Office before budgeting or filing. Professional search/advice and any international filings are additional.

## Search scope
Search exact and similar forms, including:
- QUN; QUN Mauritius; QUN Global;
- QUN Messenger, QUN Market, QUN AI, QUN ID, QUN Hub, QUN Wallet;
- the stylized Q mark, logos and phonetic/visual variants;
- relevant company names, domain names, social handles and app-store names.

## Classes to discuss with IP counsel
Likely classes depend on actual goods/services and should not be selected mechanically. Discuss at least:
- Class 9: downloadable software and apps;
- Class 35: advertising, business services and online marketplace functions;
- Class 36: financial/monetary services only if legally appropriate and actually intended;
- Class 38: telecommunications and messaging;
- Class 41: education, entertainment and publishing where relevant;
- Class 42: software/SaaS, AI and technology services.

Confirm precise wording and classification with the Industrial Property Office or qualified trademark counsel. Filing broad classes without a realistic use plan can waste money and may create obligations or vulnerabilities.

## Ownership and international consistency
- Confirm who owns the existing global QUN marks, logo assets and source files.
- Keep chain-of-title documents, contributor assignments and licences.
- Determine whether Mauritius filing should be made by the existing global owner or a local entity; do not assume the local chapter itself is a legal person.
- Ask counsel whether a national filing or Madrid System route is appropriate, considering existing registrations, designated territories and costs.
- Do not announce “trademark registered”, “protected” or “available” until supported by the official record and legal review.

## Filing decision gate
1. Complete official-register search and document similar marks.
2. Review conflicts and actual product/service descriptions with counsel.
3. Confirm applicant/owner and any needed authorization.
4. Approve budget explicitly.
5. File and track office actions/oppositions/renewals only after approval.
