# QUN Mauritius — AI agent task pack

Any AI agent can start here. Research first; do not deploy or spend money.

## Context
QUN Mauritius is a local chapter/market initiative of the single international QUN ecosystem, not a separate competing brand. Keep shared global QUN identity and code foundations where practical. Localize product and operations to Mauritius.

## Rules
- Prefer primary sources: Mauritius government, IPOM, Data Protection Office, FSC, Statistics Mauritius and official product pages.
- Cite important claims with URL and access date.
- Separate verified facts, estimates, hypotheses and recommendations.
- Never invent competitors, market gaps, users, licences or legal conclusions.
- No paid infrastructure, domain purchase, company registration, public token launch, token sale, exchange listing or financial product without explicit human approval.
- Protect secrets and personal data.
- Use feature branches and pull requests; do not push directly to main.

## Task A — competitor map
Research at least 10 services across messaging/social communities, local marketplaces/classifieds, payments, crypto/virtual assets, AI and developer tools. Record official URL, availability, languages, platforms, features, fees, onboarding, trust/safety, date, strengths, weaknesses, sources and confidence. A failed search does not prove absence. Output: update MARKET_RESEARCH.md.

## Task B — user discovery
Create an interview guide for residents, small businesses and developers. Ask about existing behaviour and concrete problems before showing QUN concepts. Avoid unnecessary personal data; include consent and anonymized-notes templates. Output: USER_DISCOVERY.md. Never fabricate interviews.

## Task C — trademark and legal research
Use official Mauritian sources to map trademark search/filing, relevant classes, operating models, data protection, marketplace/consumer obligations and VAITOS/licensing questions. Flag uncertainty for local counsel. Output: update LEGAL_COMPLIANCE.md with citations, dates and questions for counsel.

## Task D — product recommendation
Compare 2–3 pilots by demonstrated pain, reachable audience, differentiation, time to MVP, cost, legal/privacy/security risk and reuse of global QUN technology. Output: PILOT_RECOMMENDATION.md with assumptions, evidence gaps, metrics and go/no-go criteria.

## Task E — brand and localization
Create an English-first brand architecture and review workflow for French and Kreol Morisien. Treat translations as drafts until reviewed by fluent local speakers. Preserve QUN global identity. Output: update LOCALIZATION.md with glossary and review checklist.

## Definition of done
The result is useful to a human decision-maker, sources are traceable, uncertainty is explicit, and no external launch or spending has occurred. End with the next three human decisions required.
