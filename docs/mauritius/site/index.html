QUN Mauritius is intended to share the international QUN identity, technology foundations and security principles, while adapting language, support, partnerships and product priorities to Mauritius.

◈

QUN Market

Discover local businesses and products. Any first release must offer a clear benefit beyond existing marketplaces.

⌁

QUN Messenger

Communication and communities, connected to the broader ecosystem where useful — not a forced replacement for familiar apps.

✧

QUN AI

Practical tools for residents, creators and businesses, with human review and clear privacy boundaries.

◎

QUN ID

A possible shared identity layer, only when security and data-protection requirements are met.

⌘

QUN Hub

Open collaboration, developer documentation and reviewed contributions across the international project.

◇

QUN Node

Longer-term infrastructure experiments, gated by security, reliability and operational readiness.

First experiment — hypothesis

Start with one real problem.

Rather than launch a large super-app immediately, QUN could test a lightweight business workflow kit: a shareable profile/catalogue and an enquiry path for one selected group of local businesses.

What this page does not claim: no live storefront, account system, payments, token, registered local entity, approved trademark or production service is being offered here. The pilot must be selected only after competitor verification and user interviews.

Trust by design

Build carefully. Prove value.

Local voices first

Test real needs with residents and businesses; record evidence, including negative feedback.

Privacy and safety

Minimize personal data, keep demo data separate and review local obligations before launch.

Responsible rollout

No paid resources, token issuance or financial services without separate approval and legal review.
