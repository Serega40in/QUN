# QUN Launch Film — Freebeat.ai Prompt & Storyboard
## Project
**Title:** QUN — A Universe We Create Together / «QUN — Вселенная, которую создаём вместе»  
**Duration:** 75–90 seconds  
**Master:** 16:9, 4K target; prepare separate 9:16 and 1:1 edits.  
**Style:** original high-end family 3D animation, cinematic fantasy, premium tactile materials, vivid but tasteful color, expressive acting, breathtaking scale, joyful emotional storytelling.

## Master prompt (English)
“Create an original 80–90 second cinematic 3D animated brand film for QUN, a human-centered digital ecosystem. The feeling is pure wonder, joy, kindness, discovery, creative ecstasy and a beautiful future being built together. Use premium original family-animation craft, expressive character acting, sophisticated cinematic lighting, tactile soft materials, elegant camera movement, magical environmental storytelling, vivid jewel colors balanced with clean pearl-white negative space. Visual palette: pearl white, deep cosmic blue, emerald, turquoise and restrained warm gold, with controlled coral, mango and lavender accents. The mascot Q is a tiny rounded cosmic idea-gardener with short white/icy-blue plush fur, large sky-blue crystal eyes, one small emerald seed-crystal on the chest and one curved golden sprout on the head. Preserve the supplied character reference exactly in every shot.

Begin on a pure white space with a tiny luminous seed. Q discovers it; a gentle touch releases a ribbon of light that grows into a living universe. Light ribbons connect people across distances, becoming a visual metaphor for QUN Messenger. Floating sparks gather into a friendly constellation of ideas around QUN AI. Creative images and small crafted objects bloom into a vibrant community marketplace and social world. Hundreds of tiny independent device-stars join into a beautiful network constellation to represent QUN Node and the developing QUN Storage vision. One star briefly goes dark; the light route gracefully reorganizes through neighboring stars, symbolizing resilience and recovery rather than absolute guarantees. Show people learning, helping each other and creating something useful for nature and community. The camera pulls back into a breathtaking cosmic garden, where all the colorful light paths form an elegant QUN-inspired abstract emblem. End on a clean pearl-white background with the QUN wordmark area, warm golden glow, and the line: ‘QUN — создаём вместе’.

Use fluid but readable transitions, a strong visual idea per shot, carefully controlled motion blur, coherent scale, clear silhouette readability and a musical crescendo. This must feel like a complete emotional short film, not a slideshow of app screens. No generic corporate stock footage. Do not imitate any existing studio or copyrighted franchise. Do not invent readable interface text, financial claims, token prices, guaranteed rewards, absolute security claims or claims that planned products are already live. Keep all product representations symbolic unless approved UI references are supplied.”

## Freebeat workflow
1. Загрузить финальный аудиотрек из Suno.
2. Загрузить утверждённую модель Q как character reference; при возможности приложить turnaround.
3. Вставить master prompt и разбить фильм на сцены по таблице, если инструмент не удерживает консистентность длинного ролика.
4. Генерировать по 4–8 секунд на сцену; не пытаться сразу получить идеальные 90 секунд одним запросом.
5. Для каждой сцены повторять короткий character-consistency prompt из character bible.
6. Выбирать сцены с понятным действием и чистой композицией; исправлять ошибки рук, глаз, кристалла и золотого ростка.
7. Собрать монтаж по ударам и фразам финального трека, затем добавить титры отдельно в редакторе, чтобы текст не искажался генератором.
8. Экспортировать master и короткие версии; проверить мобильное кадрирование и читаемость логотипа.

## Общий negative prompt
“copyrighted character lookalikes, imitation of a specific animation studio, existing franchise costumes, distorted face, inconsistent mascot design, changing eye color, missing emerald chest crystal, missing golden sprout, extra limbs, malformed hands, jitter, flicker, abrupt camera cuts, excessive bloom, oversaturated neon, clutter, unreadable fake text, misspelled logos, watermarks, UI gibberish, weapons, horror mood, aggressive action, crypto price charts, guaranteed income claims, absolute security claims.”
